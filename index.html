Try the made-up example, or choose your task CSV.

    CSV rules

    Columns: id,task,owner,repeat,on. Owner may be blank. Extra columns are ignored.

    • daily or weekdays: leave on blank.
    • weekly: Mon|Wed, for example.
    • monthly: 1–31 or last.
    • yearly: MM-DD.
    • once: YYYY-MM-DD.

    Weekdays means Monday–Friday, including holidays. Missing days such as February 31 are skipped and reported.

    A local checklist, without shared accounts.

    Up to 100 task definitions and 20,000 completed records per backup. The app processes CSV and backups in this tab. It sends no task data to a server, creates no reminders, and does not sync between people or devices. Completion times come from your computer.
